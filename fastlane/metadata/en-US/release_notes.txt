Version 1.0 is the first release of Splicer, a watch pile for one person on this device.

• Three-page cover onboarding with Skip and Continue
• Board with Stack and Gate under Burn the gate
• Discover to search a title or pick from the shelf, then Stash on the stack
• Draw to seat one title on the gate
• Burn the gate, then rate from 1 to 5 or leave Unrated
• Ash for burn order with day and Grade or Unrated
• Profile for Burns and Grade 1 through Grade 5
• Settings with Run onboarding again, Reset all data, and Contact
• Empty board when Nothing is on the board
• Pile, burns, and grades stay on this device across launches until reset
